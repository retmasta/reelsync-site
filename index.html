reelsync

An internal automation for the Uzum Tezkor content team.

What it does

When a new Reel is published on Uzum Tezkor's own Instagram account, reelsync uploads the same video to Uzum Tezkor's own YouTube channel as a Short. The Short is scheduled to go public the next day at 12:00 Tashkent time.

Who uses it

reelsync is not a public service. Nobody outside the team signs in to it. It acts only on the brand's own Instagram account and the brand's own YouTube channel, and only with the permissions the account owners granted.

Services it uses

  • The Instagram API (Meta), to see new Reels on the brand's account.
  • YouTube API Services (Google), to upload and schedule the Shorts.

See the privacy policy for exactly what data it handles.
